# Cairn website concept

## Job

Introduce Cairn to Mac users who are short on space and wary of indiscriminate cleaner apps. Show the product before asking for trust. This is a design prototype, not a public release page.

## Design tokens

- Graphite `#101418` — page ground, continuous with the native app.
- Slate `#1A2128` — quiet grouped content.
- Chalk `#F4F7F8` — primary copy.
- Mist `#AAB8C3` — supporting copy.
- Cairn blue `#559FFF` — the primary action, borrowed from the mark.
- Stone light `#A9D9FF` — restrained focus and emphasis.

Typography: Google Fonts Instrument Sans for headings, navigation, and product copy, with the system sans as fallback. The same family unifies all three website studies. Left alignment and measured line lengths keep the page calm.

The header has no separate surface and sits over the hero. The approved four-stone icon and a very soft glow are the only hero decoration. The native screenshot carries the product proof.

## Specificity check

This avoids a generic cleaner-app promise such as “one click to reclaim gigabytes.” The page explains measured data, path-level review, protected work, recovery, and the narrow external-copy scope. It does not present a fake download or sign-up action while the app is a local development build. The name’s trail-marker meaning gets one clear sentence near the end; the hero leads with the user’s storage problem.
