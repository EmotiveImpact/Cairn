## Can I download Cairn?

Not yet. Cairn is in development for macOS. The website's app previews contain labelled sample data and have disk actions disabled.

## Will Cairn free a specific amount of space?

No fixed amount is promised. Make room can help plan toward an available-space target, but only a later capacity reading can show whether that target was reached.

## Does Cairn delete files automatically?

The normal cleanup journey uses review. The source also includes opt-in scheduled rules and optional AI-tool cleaning permissions. Scheduled rules use approved scopes and Trash only while Cairn is open. AI-tool cleaning is off by default, with a choice of approval each time or permitted eligible cleanup without a new prompt. Consent safeguards passed macOS CI on 9 November; live Mac acceptance remains open. Leave those settings off when you only want to inspect storage.

## Does moving a file to Trash reclaim its space immediately?

No. The move and the later available-space reading are separate facts. Emptying Trash or other system activity can affect what the volume reports.

## What developer tools does it cover?

The current site shows examples around Xcode build data, package caches, local models, and known coding-session locations. Coverage varies by location and scan; inspect the exact paths shown by the app. Displaying a tool logo does not imply a formal integration or endorsement.

## Can every action be undone?

No universal undo is promised. Activity & Recovery shows the options available for a recorded action. Check the specific item and its recovery details before approving a change.

## What does Pro cost?

The planned price is £35.99 once, with no trial and a lifetime licence for this major version. Scanning and one-item cleanup are intended to be free; batch cleanup is planned for Pro. Live checkout, tax treatment, device allowance and final commercial terms remain unverified. Sales are not open.

## Which Macs are targeted?

The source targets macOS 14 or later, with Apple silicon and Intel builds. Supported-Mac hardware acceptance is still outstanding.
