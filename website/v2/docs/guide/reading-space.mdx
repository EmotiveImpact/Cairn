Start in **Overview** to see the available space on your internal Mac volume. **Changes** puts dated readings beside measured developer-data locations so you can see what grew between comparable scans.

## Capacity and candidates are different

An available-space reading describes the volume at a point in time. A cleanup candidate is a specific path found by a scan. Cairn does not treat candidate sizes as a complete explanation of all occupied space, or claim to know which app wrote every byte.

## Check the evidence

- Look at the dates and volume for the readings you compare.
- Check whether the relevant scan completed and what locations it covered.
- Treat missing, denied, interrupted, or partial coverage as a gap, not as zero growth.
- Open the specialist view to inspect the actual paths before making a decision.

A later capacity reading may reflect other activity on your Mac. Cairn does not attribute every change in available space to its own actions.
