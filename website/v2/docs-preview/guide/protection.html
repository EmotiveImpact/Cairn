Keep your working state

Understand the boundary around code, sessions, credentials, and recovery data.

The files that let you resume work are not interchangeable with generated build output. Cairn treats known working locations as protected, including source-control history, coding-session locations, credentials, and recovery records.

Protection in practice

  • A repository and its history are not a routine cleanup candidate.
  • Known coding-session locations stay distinct from disposable tool caches.
  • Credentials and recovery data require their own safety boundary.
  • When Cairn cannot establish that data is safely rebuildable, it requires review rather than assuming it can go.

Protection is based on known locations and the evidence available to a scan. It is not a guarantee that every valuable file anywhere on a Mac can be identified automatically. Always inspect an unfamiliar path before approving a change.

For the developer workflow, see Review before you remove.
