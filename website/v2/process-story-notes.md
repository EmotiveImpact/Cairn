Scan: measured candidates and known working state gather into a readable view.
Review: distinguish categories and inspect paths, sizes and requirements; choose a space target.
Protect: known source control, sessions and credentials stay protected; uncertain items need review.
Verify: action record, eligible recovery and a fresh capacity reading are separate outcomes. Nothing implies automatic deletion or immediate reclaimed capacity from Trash.

## Data-to-cairn revision
The four stones now represent successive considered steps: measured understanding, reviewed choice, protected working state, verified outcome. There are 620 illustrative particles. Data streams move toward a progressively filled four-stone cairn, with blue gradients, surface highlights and an outlined boundary at the protection stage. Explanatory copy states that particles are data and stones are process steps; the diagram does not encode byte counts or depict automatic deletion. Desktop evidence captions spell out the checks at each stage.
