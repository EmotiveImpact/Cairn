# Cairn website, version two

## Purpose

Compare an atmospheric brand-led homepage with version one's direct product-led approach. Both use the approved app icon and real, labelled sample app captures. Version two should make the word *Cairn* feel natural without hiding what the macOS app does.

## Tokens and type

- Night `#0B1116` — safety section and footer.
- Ink `#192630` — text on light sections.
- Cool stone `#E1E8EC` — daylight hero backdrop.
- Paper `#F5F8F9` — product section and screenshot stage.
- Blue `#4497F9` and ice `#A6D9FF` — from the approved four-stone artwork.
- Mist `#A7B8C4` — supporting copy on dark ground.

Type: Google Fonts Instrument Sans on the daylight page and earlier studies. The active night page uses Inter Variable, with medium headings and more open tracking informed by Linear’s contact page; system sans is the fallback. Headings use one calm weight and tight spacing. The artwork and app captures remain local assets; the font loads from Google Fonts.

## Distinction from version one

Version one puts the actual app on the first long screen and explains its features in a straightforward dark utility layout. Version two opens with a cool daylight material scene against the approved dark app icon, then makes the native screenshot the dark object on a light stage. A dark alternate hero image is retained for comparison. It uses no decorative arrow glyphs in links. The art must never become a second or modified logo; the real approved icon stays in the header.

## Editorial night direction

The active page is now governed by [refinement-notes.md](refinement-notes.md). The description below records the earlier reference decisions; the current layout consolidates the repeated stages into a four-step walkthrough.

The night page takes the broad rhythm of [Save.design](https://save.design/?ref=saaspo.com): a black canvas and restrained introduction, a vivid landscape stage with a framed product preview, and hairline divisions. [Giga](https://giga.ai/) adds one concrete scenario and numbered steps with the product proving each step. [Deck](https://deck.co/?ref=saaspo.com) adds a compact sticky menu, split heading/copy intros, wide product stages and an expanded footer. [Payload](https://payloadcms.com/?ref=saaspo.com) reinforces early product proof and gives the developer-specific features priority in the secondary grid. Cairn follows an illustrative 9.9 GB to 30 GB case through Changes, Make room, review, protection, and outcome. The latest revision brings the lighter concept's four-stone image to the opening, behind the bold split headline and single flat native Overview screen. The screens open a five-view product preview dialog. The closing call to action and footer share one dark surface with light rising from the bottom and no oversized wordmark. Its blue stone artwork, approved app icon, real native sample captures, and storage-specific copy keep it specific to this product. The immediate preceding composition is preserved at `night-before-screen-hero.html` with its own stylesheet. The earlier matching-layout night page remains at `night-previous.html`; [night-story.md](night-story.md) records the complete page narrative and truth boundary.

## Truth boundary

The page says scans reveal findings, exact paths can be reviewed, and known sensitive locations are protected. It does not promise complete attribution, instant reclaimed space, one-click cleanup, external-drive migration, or a public download. App screenshots are labelled sample data.
